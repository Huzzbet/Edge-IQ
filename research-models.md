# Edge-IQ Research Models

## NRL V1
Inputs: team strength differential, home advantage, market line, scoring environment, rest/fatigue and availability adjustments.
Outputs: win probability and fair price.
Status: DEVELOPMENT / PAPER ONLY.

## Tennis V1
Inputs: player rating differential, surface adjustment, serve/return strength, recent form, fatigue and rest.
Outputs: match win probability and fair price.
Status: DEVELOPMENT / PAPER ONLY.

## EPL V1
Planned Poisson goal model using attack/defence strength, home advantage and team news.
Status: DEVELOPMENT / PAPER ONLY.

## Promotion gates
No model is promoted to real-money use without sufficient out-of-sample predictions, paper-bet sample, calibration evidence, positive CLV, positive ROI, drawdown review and market-quality review.
