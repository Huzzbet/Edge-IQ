Independent probability. Fair price. Measured edge.

MODEL EV

Model EV is calculated from an independent predictive probability and the best available price: probability × odds − 1. Fair odds are the inverse of the model probability.

BASELINE SCREENING

When an independent probability is unavailable, Edge-IQ may use bookmaker consensus to identify market anomalies and research candidates. These are labelled BASELINE and are not presented as genuine model EV.

AFL MODEL

AFL-V1.0 is an independent statistical research layer using team performance strength, ladder percentage, win strength and home advantage, with conservative uncertainty assumptions. It does not read bookmaker odds when forming its probability.

VALIDATION GATES

Predictions are captured for later comparison with closing prices and results. Promotion toward a validated edge requires meaningful sample size plus positive calibration/CLV/ROI evidence and acceptable drawdown. Until then, signals remain research opportunities.
