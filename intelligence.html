Decision layer

From market noise to ranked opportunities.

Edge-IQ separates independent model probability from market screening, then ranks opportunities using price, confidence, coverage and historical evidence.

Great OpportunityStrong positive model EV with supporting confidence and market quality.
Value PlayAttractive price relative to the model, without overstating certainty.
Risk-AdjustedBalances expected value against uncertainty, coverage and available evidence.

How to use the scanner

Start with the highest-ranked opportunities, verify the underlying event and price, then use the Research/CLV evidence before increasing stake size. Baseline screening is deliberately separated from genuine model EV.

Research discipline

A theoretical edge is not the same thing as a proven edge. Edge-IQ records predictions and market movement so calibration, closing-line value, ROI, drawdown and sample-size gates can be evaluated over time.
