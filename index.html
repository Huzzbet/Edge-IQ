Model architecture

Edge-IQ accepts an external probability on each market when supplied by a future statistical model/API. Until that feed exists, it uses a conservative BASELINE probability derived from de-vig consensus and shrunk toward 50%. This prevents the app from falsely claiming that bookmaker consensus is an independent predictive model.
